Why This Course Is Different

Deep, Not a Module-Wide Detour

Sibling TechNaom courses touch LLM security in at most one module or chapter. This course goes deep across a full 13 chapters — the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, real jailbreak techniques, data poisoning, model extraction, and more.

Every Attack Paired With a Real Defense

No attack technique is taught without a real, working mitigation shown alongside it — defensive and educational throughout, never an unsolved cliffhanger.

Tested Against Real, Running Models — Honestly Disclosed When It Isn't

Every attack demonstration is verified against a real, installed model whenever possible before it's written into a lesson — and every chapter's quality audit discloses exactly what was live-tested versus logically-sound-but-unverified, never overclaiming. An untested claim presented as tested is worse than useless in a security course.
