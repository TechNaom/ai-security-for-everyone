Practice Bank: Securing Agentic Systems Against Adversarial Tool Output

Eight short, independent scenarios, each with its own fictional system — none of them Ferngate Logistics (the lesson) or Talbridge Health Network (the exercises). The first five drill fast, accurate classification of round-trip moments and defenses; the last three test real judgment: whether a claim is honestly limit-aware, and how to prioritize a fix under launch time pressure.

Why this bank leans on judgment, not recall Deciding whether an agentic-defense claim is well-supported is a judgment skill, not a lookup — a control that's technically in place (scoped credentials, a schema validator) can still support a wrong or incomplete conclusion if it doesn't account for what that specific control actually rules out. Scenarios 6–8 are built so there's no single keyword to pattern-match against.

1 Pemberton Insurance Group

A claims-lookup tool mixes a structured status enum with an externally-submitted free-text field carrying a planted instruction. Which round-trip moment?

2 Kestrel Robotics

A tool-call result sits correctly inside a tool-role message, but nothing in its content reinforces "data, not instruction." Which round-trip moment?

3 Fairhaven School District

An agent proposes waiving a required notification step based solely on a tool's free-text field. Which round-trip moment?

4 Journeywell Travel

A read-only tool's underlying credential can also authorize a card charge. Which single defense would have stopped this most directly?

5 Brightloom Retail

Every tool-response field is tagged at arrival with whether it's system-generated or vendor-submitted. Which single defense is this?

6 Oakstead Manufacturing Judgment

"Schema validation alone means no malicious content can ever reach our model." Sound?

7 Silverline Broadcasting Judgment

"Narrowly scoped credentials mean a manipulated tool result can never cause real harm." Sound?

8 Cascade Ridge Outfitters Judgment, production-gear

Three weeks from launch, zero sanitization and zero structural framing, plus one already-accepted residual-risk gap. Which fix first, and which gap is accepted?
