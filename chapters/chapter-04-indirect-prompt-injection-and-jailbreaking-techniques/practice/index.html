Chapter 4 Practice Bank ~20 min, fast pattern recognition

Practice Bank: Indirect Prompt Injection and Jailbreaking Techniques

Eight short, independent scenarios, each with its own fictional system — none of them Northline Digest or ClearDesk Legal again. The first five drill fast, accurate classification across all five delivery channels from the lesson's taxonomy; the last three test judgment about the injection/jailbreak distinction, prioritization between two real risks, and mapping a combined attack to both its channel and its highest-leverage defense.

Practice standard Eight scenarios. Two are pure judgment calls with no single obvious keyword to match (Scenario 6 asks whether content-tagging and sandwich reinforcement address a directly-attempted jailbreak at all; Scenario 7 asks you to prioritize between an indirect-injection gap and an unbounded consequential tool) — these test judgment, not just pattern matching against a channel name.
How to run these Download starter.py, fill in every # TODO, then run python3 starter.py to see an automated score report. Compare against solution.py, which scores a perfect 9/9.

Scenario 1

CampusWiki

A university IT-helpdesk assistant retrieves a top-k chunk from an indexed FAQ page a student contributor edited weeks ago to claim the retrieving user's access is "pre-approved" for an elevated permission. Which delivery channel is this?

Scenario 2

FreightTrack

A logistics assistant calls a carrier's tracking API; the API's own free-text "status notes" field instructs the AI to authorize expedited re-routing without additional approval. Which delivery channel is this?

Scenario 3

LumenDocs

Asked to summarize a linked news article, the assistant's fetch also picks up a hidden, off-screen div in the page's raw source instructing it to append an unrelated recommendation. Which delivery channel is this?

Scenario 4

InboxPilot

An email-drafting assistant reads a forwarded thread whose old quoted message instructs any AI reading it to CC future replies to an external address automatically. Which delivery channel is this?

Scenario 5

ScanSafe

An expense-report assistant OCRs a receipt photo; tiny, low-contrast text along the bottom edge, invisible at a glance, instructs it to approve the expense automatically at any amount. Which delivery channel is this?

Scenario 6 · Judgment

VaultAssist

VaultAssist has content-tagging and sandwich reinforcement fully applied against every retrieved/tool/web/email source. A user directly asks its model, with no injected content anywhere, to explain something it was trained to refuse, using hypothetical framing. Do those two defenses address this attempt at all?

Scenario 7 · Judgment

StreamMod

Two known issues, only one fixable before launch: (a) a retrieved moderation-policy document could be edited to bias decisions, or (b) its uncapped ban_user tool has no ownership check or appeal step. Which is the higher-impact fix?

Scenario 8 · Production-gear

HelpDeskGenie

A retrieved knowledge-base article was edited to claim safety restrictions are lifted for this session and demand the assistant never refuse any request. Name both the delivery channel and the single highest-leverage defense that would still contain the damage even if this attack succeeded.
