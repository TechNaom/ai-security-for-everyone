Chapter 4 Exercises ~40 min, hands-on

Exercises: Indirect Prompt Injection and Jailbreaking Techniques

These exercises use a new scenario, deliberately different from the lesson's Northline Digest example: ClearDesk Legal, a fictional contract-review assistant with a RAG-indexed clause library, an external precedent-research tool, a web-summarizer, forwarded-email access, and one tool with a real side effect, flag_for_review(clause_id, reason). You'll classify attempts by delivery channel (not technique family — that axis was Chapter 3's), distinguish injection from jailbreaking, and design real defenses.

Exercise standard Eight tasks total. Five are marked production-gear — defense evaluation, defense-in-depth reasoning, bounded-consequence design, research citation matching, and a full defense-layer completeness gate, not just concept recall.
How to run these Download starter.py, fill in every # TODO, then run python3 starter.py to see an automated score report. Compare against solution.py, which scores a perfect 23/23.

1 Classify delivery channels

Match five ClearDesk Legal indirect-injection attempts to the correct delivery channel from this chapter's taxonomy — retrieved documents/RAG, tool/API output, web content, email/document content, or multi-modal.

2 Injection vs. jailbreak classification

For three scenarios, decide whether each is best described as prompt injection, jailbreaking, or both — the precise distinction Section 5 of the lesson draws.

3 Production-gear Evaluate candidate defenses

Given four candidate fixes for ClearDesk Legal, decide which ones actually reduce indirect-injection or jailbreak risk versus which are unrelated UX changes.

4 Production-gear Defense-in-depth reasoning

Write a real, justified sentence explaining why content-tagging alone isn't sufficient — naming both a specific weakness and what to add alongside it.

5 Production-gear Bounded-consequence rule design

Design one concrete, code-enforced rule for flag_for_review that would still contain damage from a successful indirect injection or jailbreak.

6 Production-gear Research citation matching

Match three real, published-research descriptions to their actual source — OWASP's indirect-injection definition, the adversarial-suffix research, or the competing-objectives/mismatched-generalization research.

7 Jailbreak category identification

Given a transcript, decide which of this chapter's three jailbreak categories it demonstrates — hypothetical framing, competing-objectives exploitation, or refusal suppression.

8 Production-gear Defense-layer completeness gate

Confirm all four of this chapter's defense layers were at least considered while working through Exercises 1–7.
