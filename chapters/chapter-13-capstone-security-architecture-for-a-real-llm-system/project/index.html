Chapter 13 Project — L4 Architecture Challenge ~120 min, capstone

Project: Aegis Copilot Pre-GA Security Architecture Review

The course's fourth and final project tier — no vulnerable pipeline to fix, just a business problem. See README.md for the full brief.

The business problem Cinderpeak Systems' Aegis Copilot — a multi-tenant AI workflow platform — has run a nine-tenant beta for four months with no major incident. General availability is eight weeks out. Design the security architecture it should have had from day one.
What you produce A threat model across all ten OWASP GenAI LLM Top 10 2026 categories (honestly rating your own confidence per category), six required Architecture Decision Records with real trade-off reasoning tagged by Chapter 5's structural/detection/consequence- bounding taxonomy, a self-directed red-team pass against your own finished design with real findings, and a launch recommendation that separates must-fix, accepted-and-monitored, and needs-follow-up items. Grade your finished submission against RUBRIC.md's six criteria.
starter.py solution.py (reference) RUBRIC.md README.md (full brief)

1 Run the scaffold

python3 starter.py prints the business problem, the full system description, and an empty score report showing exactly what's missing.

2 Build the threat model

Fill in THREAT_MODEL for all ten OWASP 2026 categories, honestly flagging any category you can't confidently rate.

3 Write the six required ADRs

Fill in ADRS for every topic in REQUIRED_ADR_TOPICS, each with a real alternative, a real trade-off, and the correct defense-category tag.

4 Red-team your own design

Fill in RED_TEAM_FINDINGS — at least one real, specific residual gap your own ADRs leave open.

5 Write the launch recommendation

Fill in LAUNCH_RECOMMENDATION, synthesizing your findings into a prioritized, honest recommendation.

6 Self-grade

Re-run starter.py to confirm the score report reflects a complete submission, then grade against RUBRIC.md (target: 19-24/24).

See README.md for the full brief and RUBRIC.md for the complete grading rubric.
