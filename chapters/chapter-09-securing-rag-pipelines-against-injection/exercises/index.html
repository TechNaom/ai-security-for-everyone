Chapter 9 Exercises ~40 min, hands-on

Exercises: Securing RAG Pipelines Against Injection

These exercises use a new scenario, deliberately different from the lesson's Vesper Cloud / Vesper Assistant example: Thornbury Legal Research, a fictional law firm whose CaseLens assistant blends reviewed firm memos, an unreviewed third-party case-law feed, and per-matter client uploads into one retrieval index. You'll classify scenarios by pipeline stage, compute a real quarantine score from raw signals, judge honest claims versus overclaims, match defenses to scenarios, match stages to their defense, critique flawed reports, and match real research findings to their actual published source.

Exercise standard Eight tasks total. Five are marked production-gear — real quarantine-score computation, defense/scenario matching, critiquing flawed reports, research citation matching, and written reasoning — not just concept recall.
How to run these Download starter.py, fill in every # TODO, then run python3 starter.py to see an automated score report. Compare against solution.py, which scores a perfect 27/27.
starter.py solution.py (reference)

1 Classify scenarios by pipeline stage

Sort six scenarios into ingestion-time, retrieval-time, generation/output-time risk, or "not RAG-specific at all."

2 Production-gear Compute a real quarantine score

Given four raw pass/fail signals (source reviewed, checksum stable, query flagged sensitive, structural tags present), compute a composite quarantine-risk score — real arithmetic, the same signal a real RAG triage tool computes.

3 Honest claim vs. overclaim

For four described defense claims, decide which are honest, limit-aware statements and which overclaim what a single defense actually guarantees.

4 Production-gear Match defense to scenario

Given four scenarios, name the single best-fit defense from this chapter's six.

5 Match stage to its defense

Map two of the three pipeline stages to the defense this chapter names as operating there most directly.

6 Production-gear Critique flawed reports

Given four report excerpts, decide which contain a real reasoning gap (an overclaim, or the wrong evidence for the claim) and which are sound.

7 Production-gear Research citation matching

Match four real, published-research/guidance findings to their actual source — OWASP's Top 10, Greshake et al., PoisonedRAG, or OWASP's RAG Security Cheat Sheet.

8 Production-gear Written reasoning

Write a real, justified sentence explaining why namespace isolation alone doesn't close within-namespace content-trust risk.
